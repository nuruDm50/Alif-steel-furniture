About Us

Home › About Us

Bangladesh's Most Trusted Steel Furniture Brand

Since 2010, Alif Steel Furniture has been manufacturing premium quality steel wardrobes and almirahs for homes and offices across Bangladesh. Starting from a small workshop in Dhaka, we have grown into a nationwide brand trusted by over 50,000 families.

Our products are crafted from high-grade CR steel with advanced powder coating technology, ensuring rust-resistance, durability, and a beautiful finish that lasts for generations.
